AS690 CIDR Squeezings Report:  7204 Nets, 95 ASs, 1414 Aggregates
-----------------------------------------------------------------

7204 (79%) of the ever-announced more-specific routes within aggregates have
     been withdrawn.  579 of those were withdrawn within the last week.
                      265 the week before that.
                      74 the week before that.

  95 ASs have registered aggregates in the PRDB.
  86 of those are announcing aggregates.
  49 have withdrawn at least one more specific route.

1414 Aggregates are configured.
1227 of these were Top-Level Aggregates (not nested in another aggregate).
1007 of these are being announced to AS690.
 838 of those have at least one subnet configured (the other 169 may be saving
                                 the Internet future subnet announcements).
 729 have stopped announcing at least one configured more specific route.
 704 have stopped announcing half of their configured more specific routes.
 679 have stopped announcing most (80%) of their more specific routes.

See merit.edu:pub/nsfnet/cidr/cidr_savings for more detail.

REPORT CHANGES:                                     (Updated May 31, 1994)

Anyone considering configuring an aggregate into the PRDB (and you all
should be!) is encouraged to pre-check that aggregate by typing the command:

		  whois -h prdb.merit.edu 'aggchk <agg>'

(where "<agg>" is the aggregate description).  This command will list
all of the other entries in the PRDB that are more specific routes of <agg>,
as well as any aggregates all ready configured that contain <agg>.  The 
output includes the AUP and announcement lists of each of the nets printed, 
with discrepancies flagged.  This is the same program that we use for 
sanity-checking the NACRs that you submit.


The archived discussion list "db-disc@merit.edu" exists for discussion of 
PRDB issues.  Send a message to "db-disc-request@merit.edu" to subscribe.
						--Dale Johnson (dsj@merit.edu)
