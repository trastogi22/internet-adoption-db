# Internet Adoption Database — Work-in-Progress Update
**Date:** 2026-10-18
**Author:** Tanya Rastogi
**Status:** Two sources incorporated end-to-end; pipeline working; key data-quality
caveats identified.

---

## 1. Summary

I built a reproducible pipeline (download → clean → aggregate → analyze) and
incorporated two contrasting sources:

| Source | Network(s) | Coverage | Firms extracted | Evidence strength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IETF Internet Monthly Reports | JvNCnet (regional IP) | Oct 1992 (1 report) | 9 | High ("new on-line members, fully operational") |
| UUCP Mapping Project (encse/uucp-map) | UUCP (mail) | 1987–1992 | ~8,200 unique | Medium/Low (map appearance) |

Combined dataset: **~8,600 observations → ~8,200 unique firms**, with a derived
"first connection" per firm. Outputs, figures, and summary tables are all
generated by code and committed.

---

## 2. Process (per source)

**IETF Monthly Reports.** Saved the raw report text unaltered. Wrote a parser
that locates "new on-line members / operational" sections, extracts the
organizations listed beneath them, and uses the **reporting month from the
section header** (not the posting date). A parenthetical override date on an
entry (e.g., Trenton "Sept. 1992") takes precedence. All 9 firms hand-verified
against the raw text.

**UUCP Maps.** Cloned the repo (commit hash recorded in the manifest). Wrote a
parser for the `#N`/`#O`/`#P` record format, taking the **date from the file's
ISO timestamp, not the `#W` edit field** (which records when an entry was edited,
not when a node connected). Filtered out universities and obvious junk, then
deduplicated each organization to its **earliest appearance** in the available
snapshots. Hand-reviewed a 25-row random sample.

---

## 3. Key decisions

- **Data model:** one row per firm-per-network-observation. "First connection"
  is *derived* in analysis, not hard-coded at data entry — so a firm can appear
  in multiple networks and we can revise the definition later.
- **Network categories kept separate:** UUCP (mail) is tagged distinctly from
  regional IP, because a UUCP mail connection is not a TCP/IP internet connection.
- **Faithful extraction + layered interpretation:** raw files are never edited;
  cleaning, notes, and flags are all applied reproducibly in code.
- **Dates standardized to YYYY-MM** with a `date_precision` field.
- **Confidence field:** IETF = high; UUCP = medium/low depending on corporate
  signals in the name.

## 4. Findings

- The two sources have **essentially no firm overlap** (0 cross-source matches).
  This is expected: IETF's 9 are small regional-IP firms (Oct 1992); UUCP's are
  mostly tech companies on mail (1987–1992). I verified the name-matching logic
  would catch a match if one existed (e.g., "Sun Microsystems, Inc." and
  "Sun Microsystems" both normalize to the same key).
- UUCP surfaces recognizable early adopters: Sun Microsystems, Texas Instruments,
  Xerox, Motorola, Hewlett-Packard, Pratt & Whitney, ITT, National Semiconductor.

## 5. Data-quality caveats (important)

- **UUCP dates = "first observed in available maps," not verified first
  connection.** Snapshot coverage is very uneven:
  1987: 418 files · 1988: 1,572 · **1989: 2 · 1990: 3** · 1991: 317 · 1992: 2,389.
  So 1989–1990 are effectively missing, and firms first connecting then are
  mis-dated to a later snapshot. The year distribution reflects *snapshot
  availability* as much as actual adoption. UUCP dates should be read as upper
  bounds / "observed-by" dates.
- **Left-censoring:** firms already on UUCP before the first map (Apr 1987) all
  collapse to 1987-04; their true first date is unknown.
- **Firm filter has ~15–25% false positives.** In a 25-row hand review: ~15 clear
  companies, ~4 divisions/projects of real firms (e.g., separate rows for Xerox
  and HP divisions), ~6 non-firms (a high school, placeholder text like
  "Not Organized", and likely hobbyist BBSes). I added filters for school terms
  and junk placeholders; BBSes remain hard to distinguish automatically.
- **Deduplication is by normalized exact name**, so divisions/variants (e.g.,
  "Texas Instruments" vs "Texas Instruments; Process Automation Center") still
  count separately. Parent-company consolidation (fuzzy matching) is future work.
- **IETF scope note:** only the JvNCnet section of one report lists named
  organizations; other regional networks that month report only aggregate growth
  ("grew by N members") without names.

## 6. Open questions for you

1. **Scope of "firm":** should non-private entities the sources list alongside
   companies (school districts, training institutes, non-profits) be included,
   excluded, or flagged separately?
2. **UUCP date interpretation:** is "first observed in available maps" (with the
   coverage caveat) an acceptable measure, or should I restrict to well-covered
   years, or seek additional map archives to fill 1989–1990?
3. **Entity granularity:** keep divisions/projects as separate observations, or
   consolidate to parent firms? (Affects counts substantially.)
4. **Target definition:** is the goal first connection to *any* predecessor
   network, or specifically first *IP/internet* connection? This changes how
   UUCP is used.
